The SS26 Bioinformatics Practical demos (students port MATLAB to Julia; the use-case files are listed in the project memory) as real-world fixtures, and a "Coming from MATLAB" page mapping `seqpdist` / `seqneighjoin` / `seqlinkage` / `plot(tree)` to treescape in Python and Julia. ## Performance

- **Faster neighbor joining (v0.7, decided 2026-09-21).** v0.6's NJ is the exact O(n³) algorithm with the pinned left-to-right row totals, 2.0–3.7× slower than scikit-bio at 500–2000 taxa (UPGMA, with cached row minima, is 2.3–3.9× slower than SciPy). Options, each a convention change or a new claim: maintain row totals incrementally (changes the pinned summation order, so the reference changes with it); RapidNJ-style bounds on Q to skip most of the scan; a nearest-neighbour chain for UPGMA (tie behaviour must be re-pinned). v0.6 already keeps three optimizations that change no result: an active-order compacted matrix and overlapped row totals for NJ, and cached row minima for UPGMA.


- **Sub-quadratic branch styling.** Metadata-driven branch styling is O(nodes × depth) (~0.3 s at 8k tips on a ladder tree). A faster algorithm must preserve the pinned left-to-right Neumaier summation per subtree.
